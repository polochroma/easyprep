Bungee Safety Challenge

You are the safety engineer. Your jumper is a toy, your cord is a chain of rubber bands, and the platform is 3.00 m up. Calibrate the cord, use conservation of energy to predict the stop point, and build a cord that stops the jumper within 5 cm of the floor without touching it. You get ONE official drop.

Your name (first and last): it goes on your safety report.
