Rocket Boat

Airbags after a government crash test (NHTSA photo, public domain).

Look at the photo. Those airbags filled in about 0.03 seconds from a solid chemical that turned into gas. Too little gas and the landing is too hard; too much and the bag inflates too hard. The amount is set ahead of time, so there is ONE chance to get it right. Now you are the engineer: a dropper bottle with vinegar, baking soda in the cap, and a gutter full of water. Use stoichiometry to make just enough gas to stop the boat inside the landing zone.
