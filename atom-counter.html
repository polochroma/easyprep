Atom counter

Atoms are not made or lost in a reaction. Every element needs the SAME count going in and coming out.

Two kinds of numbers

Big number in front (coefficient): how many of that particle. This is the one you change.
Small number (subscript): how many atoms are inside one particle. It is part of the formula, so it stays.
